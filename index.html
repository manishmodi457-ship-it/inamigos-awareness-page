Our Story

Where society sees a problem, we see a life waiting to be uplifted.

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla, the InAmigos Foundation operates out of Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, reaching across the entirety of India.

We don't just talk about change; we roll up our sleeves and create it. Whether it's ensuring a child gets basic education, empowering a woman to be financially independent, or feeding a hungry stray animal—our mission is driven by deep empathy and collective action.

Core Pillars of Action:

  • Eradicating hunger and providing basic necessities.
  • Bridging educational and digital literacy gaps.
  • Environmental sustainability & Animal welfare.
